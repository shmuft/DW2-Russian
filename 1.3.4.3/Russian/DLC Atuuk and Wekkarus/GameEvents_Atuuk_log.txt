->

Description: A very long time ago, we Atuuk lived here on our world and only had each other for company. We didn't know we were lonely, because we didn't know there were nice aliens out there. Then came the Long-Ago Friends, who sent us the Big Gift: spaceships and computers and all kinds of interesting, mysterious things, and they all fell from the sky! It took us many years to reverse engineer the gifts of the Long-Ago Friends. Sometimes we understood these gifts quickly. Other times a few fires and explosions taught us that we had to rethink our assumptions.

Why did the Long-Ago Friends choose to greet us by sending their ships hurling to the ground in balls of flame? No one knows for sure, but our greatest minds believe that they wanted the Atuuk to experience the satisfaction of solving complex puzzles, such as how to make a broken ship fly.

And figure it out is exactly what we did! We studied and took apart the gifts from the Long-Ago Friends. The machines were complicated, and maybe we didn't always get everything exactly right, but we understood enough to reach the stars. We had one great mission: to find the Long-Ago Friends and thank them for the Big Gift.

But then there was a terrible war. Some aliens, who were not friendly, hurt a lot of people and destroyed many worlds. They broke space itself, which is hard to do and also embarrassing. We Atuuk, who had only just begun our journey to the stars, suffered a bit of a setback.

Finally, after many hundreds of years dedicated to problem-solving and big-thinking, we have returned to where we were before the Cataclysm. We are ready to visit the stars, and we are ready to resume our search for the Long-Ago Friends.

Who were they? Why did they give us the Big Gift of exploding ships and smashed computers? So many mysteries await, but we will find all the answers. We will learn who the Long-Ago Friends are, and we will visit them, and we will give them a long overdue hug.

Friendship awaits!

Дружба ждёт!
